# PhishGuard: Fake Offer Letter & Job Scam Inspector

> **AI & Forensic OSINT Engine to detect fake job offers, check scams, and domain spoofing in seconds.**

## What it Does

PhishGuard analyzes job offer letters, emails, and contracts to detect recruitment fraud before candidates lose money or identity data.

- **0–100% Fraud Risk Gauge**: Instant verdict (**Legitimate / Suspicious / Critical Risk**) with SHA-256 case verification.
- **Domain & Email OSINT**: Checks lookalike domains (e.g. `@google-careers-portal.com`), WHOIS age (<30 days), and SPF/DKIM authentication.
- **Document & Metadata Audit**: Identifies template software (Canva/Word converters vs corporate publishers) and digital PKI signatures.
- **Behavioral Scam Detection**: Flags fake cashier checks, P2P wire transfers (Zelle), zero-interview hiring, and Telegram redirects.
- **Salary Sanity Benchmark**: Catches unrealistic compensation lures vs market medians.
- **Interactive X-Ray Split-Pane**: Visual color-coded highlighting of fraud triggers mapped to MITRE ATT&CK & FTC codes.
- **Recharts Risk Timeline**: Visualizes whether incoming offers are escalating in danger over time.
- **One-Click Reporting**: Exports official PDF incident reports and pre-fills complaints for FBI IC3 and FTC.
